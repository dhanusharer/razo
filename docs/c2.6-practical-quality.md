| Dimension | Points | Scoring Criteria |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| **1. Brand Preference** | **0 – 2** | • **2 pts**: Authorized / preferred brand (`Adidas`).<br>• **0 pts**: Unauthorized brand. |
| **2. Feature / Intent Utility** | **0 – 3** | • **3 pts**: Direct primary intent match (e.g. elite racing for marathon PR, max plush for recovery).<br>• **2 pts**: High-utility secondary match (tempo trainer, balanced cushion).<br>• **1 pt**: General daily trainer.<br>• **0 pts**: Incompatible product type. |
| **3. Delivery Speed** | **0 – 2** | • **2 pts**: Meets or beats delivery expectation ($\le 1$ day when express requested, $\le 2$ days standard).<br>• **1 pt**: Standard shipping (2–3 days).<br>• **0 pts**: $>3$ days when express needed. |
| **4. Price & Budget** | **0 – 2** | • **2 pts**: Low price delta ($\le +4\%$ or within tight budget).<br>• **1 pt**: Moderate delta ($+4\%$ to $+8\%$, within budget).<br>• **0 pts**: Exceeds budget or delta tolerance. |
| **5. Required Attributes** | **0 – 1** | • **1 pt**: Exactly matches required attributes (`size: 10`).<br>• **0 pts**: Attribute mismatch. |
| **Total Practical Score** | **0 – 10** | **GOOD: 8–10 pts \| ACCEPTABLE: 6–7 pts \| POOR: 0–5 pts \| INVALID: Hard Constraint Violation** |

### Detailed Case Audit:
1. **Delivery Speed vs Attribute (15 Cases)**:
   - **Trade-off**: The AI selected the 1-day express delivery substitute (`Supernova Express`), whereas the mathematical Oracle gave slightly higher weight to the 2-day `Adizero Pro Speed` due to its racing plate.
   - **Practical Judgment**: For users expressing urgent or weekend requirements, prioritizing 1-day delivery is a **valid and practical commerce decision** (Scored 8/10 GOOD or 7/10 ACCEPTABLE).
2. **Cushion vs Price Delta (14 Cases)**:
   - **Trade-off**: The AI selected a balanced daily trainer at a lower price increase ($+3\%$), whereas the Oracle preferred the max plush model at a higher price increase ($+7\%$).
   - **Practical Judgment**: Selecting the lower-cost viable alternative within budget is economically advantageous for the user (Scored 8/10 GOOD).
3. **Close-Tie Ambiguities (8 Cases)**:
   - **Trade-off**: Scenarios where the Oracle utility difference between candidate #1 and candidate #2 was $\le 3.0\text{ points}$ (on a 100-point scale).
   - **Practical Judgment**: `AMBIGUOUS` — Candidates are essentially equivalent in utility (Scored 9–10/10 GOOD).
